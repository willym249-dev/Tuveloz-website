Please take your time with this, and ask us about anything that is not clear — that is what it is for. It is one page, in plain words, so that nothing here can surprise you later. There is no fee agreement because there is no fee.

1. This is free, and it stays free unless you agree otherwise in writing

No charge, no percentage, no deposit, no card, no success fee. Not now, and not later without a separate written agreement that you see first, sign first, and are free to say no to. Saying no costs you nothing and we will not make it awkward.

If you qualify for hospital financial assistance, we will never take a share of it. That is yours by law. We will also tell you about the services that do this free — Dollar For for assistance applications, and the Maryland Attorney General's Health Education and Advocacy Unit for billing and insurance disputes.

2. We do the reading. You stay in charge

We review your bills and write up what is worth questioning, with the source for each point. You read it, approve it, and send it in your own name. You make the calls, and we will give you a script and stay on the line if you want one.

We do not speak for you. We are not your representative, we are not your agent, and we will not contact a hospital, a plan, or a collector on your behalf. If that sounds like less help, it is not — it means nothing goes out with your name on it that you have not read and agreed with first.

3. What we are not

  • Not lawyers. Nothing we give you is legal advice.
  • Not doctors. We do not review whether your care was appropriate.
  • We do not negotiate, settle, or reduce any debt with anyone. Whether this service nonetheless falls under Maryland's debt settlement rules is an open question we have put in writing to the regulator — we are not charging anyone and not acting for anyone until it is answered.
  • Not certified medical coders. Some questions on a bill need your chart, the payer's own policy, and a qualified coder — we will tell you which of our points are settled and which are questions that need someone else.

4. What we can and cannot tell you from a bill

A bill on its own can show arithmetic that does not add up, dates that fall outside your stay, quantities that look wrong, and charges that do not match your insurer's statement. It cannot establish that a service was medically unnecessary, that a code was wrongly chosen, or that something never happened. Those need records we do not have. Anything in that second category leaves here as a question, never as a finding.

5. Your records — what we commit to

Kept encrypted, on equipment we control.

Seen only by the people named on this form as working your file.

Never sold, rented, or shared for marketing — ever, by anyone, for any reason.

Never used as an example, a case study, or a screenshot, even anonymously, unless you agree separately in writing.

Destroyed _______ months after we finish (proposed: 24), or sooner if you ask in writing. If something on your account is still in dispute, we hold them until it closes and then destroy them.

Returned or deleted at any time on your written request. You do not have to give a reason.

To do the review we use software tools. Before any bill goes into one, we remove your name, date of birth, address, record number, account number, and insurance ID and replace them with your file number. This reduces the risk considerably. It does not make the information anonymous, and we do not claim it does — exact dates and unusual procedures can still narrow things down. If you would rather we did not use those tools at all, say so and we will work on paper.

6. Things we will send you elsewhere

If any of these apply, we will say so immediately and point you toward proper help rather than taking the file: a lawsuit, judgment, garnishment, lien, or court date; bankruptcy or an estate; workers' compensation or an injury claim; a deadline less than a week away; a denial that turns on medical necessity; a matter involving a minor or someone under guardianship; records of psychiatric or substance-use treatment; or a bill from a facility our staff work at.

7. You can stop this at any time

You can end this whenever you like, in writing, for any reason or none, and nothing is owed — there is nothing to owe. You do not have to explain yourself. If we have to stop, we will tell you plainly why, return or destroy your records however you prefer, and give you everything already prepared so you can carry on without us.

8. No promises about the outcome

We cannot promise a reduction, an approved application, or a successful appeal — hospitals and insurers decide those, not us, and anyone who promises you otherwise is not being straight with you. What we do promise is the work described here, done carefully, and plain honesty about what we do not know. Sometimes the honest answer is that the bill is correct, and we will tell you that too.
