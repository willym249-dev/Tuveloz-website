# Letter templates — written for the consumer to sign and send

Six letters, in the order they go out. They are deliberately calm and businesslike — the person opening them did not cause this, and a letter that reads as an accusation gets filed rather than actioned. Warmth belongs in how you talk to the client; clarity belongs in what goes to the billing office. **Every one is written in the consumer's own voice, signed by them, and sent by them.** You prepare the draft; they read it, change anything they want, sign it, and post it. Nothing here is sent by you, on anyone's behalf, or over anyone else's signature.

Every phone call gets a written follow-up the same day — the letter is what exists in six months when the person who took the call has left.

**Language discipline, on every one of these.** Each item is a **question about a possible discrepancy**, never an assertion that a charge is wrong. A bill alone cannot establish that a service was upcoded, wrongly unbundled, medically unnecessary, or never provided — those need the record, the payer's policy, and qualified review. Ask for a check; do not declare a finding.

Nothing here asks anyone to accept less than what is owed. **No letter negotiates, settles, or seeks a reduction of a debt.** Whether this model requires registration under Maryland's Debt Settlement Services Act is **unresolved — the question for the regulator is drafted and not yet sent** — do not describe the wording as putting anything outside that Act.

Send by a method that produces proof of delivery on anything that matters. Attach nothing. The consumer writes about their own account and needs no authorization to do so.

---

## 1 — Itemized bill request

Sent by the consumer on every account, before any analysis.

> [Date]
>
> [Facility name] — Patient Financial Services
> [Address]
>
> **Re: Request for itemized statement — Account [number], [patient name], DOB [date]**
>
> I am the patient named above and I am writing about my own account.
>
> Please provide a **fully itemized statement** for the services rendered on [dates of service], including revenue codes, CPT and HCPCS codes, units, quantities, dates of service for each line, and the UB-04 as submitted. A summary statement is not sufficient for this review.
>
> Please also tell me whether the account can be noted as under review, and what that does or does not do about collection activity while I wait.
>
> Please confirm in writing when the itemization has been sent and the date it was dispatched.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Your name]
> [Contact]
>
>

## 2 — Candidate-question packet

The main one. Prepared only after the review, and sent by the consumer. One packet per billing entity.

> [Date]
>
> **Re: Disputed charges — Account [number], [patient name], DOB [date], dates of service [dates]**
>
> I have gone through the itemized statement dated [date] line by line, and I have questions about the entries below. I am not disputing the account generally, and I am not asserting that any of these is wrong — I am asking you to check each one.
>
> | Line | Code | Date | Charge | Why it appears incorrect |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | [n] | [code] | [date] | $[amt] | [e.g. I was discharged the morning of [date] — please check whether a room charge for that date belongs here] |
> | [n] | [code] | [date] | $[amt] | [e.g. Codes [X] and [Y] are billed separately — please confirm whether they should fall under [Z]] |
> | [n] | [code] | [date] | $[amt] | [e.g. The quantity billed is [n] — please check that against the record] |
>
> **What I am asking for:** please route this account to **coding review and charge integrity**, correct anything found to be in error, and send me a corrected itemized statement. If a line is correct as billed, please tell me why so I understand it.
>
> Please tell me in writing what protection, if any, this review gives me against collection activity while it is open, and what I would need to do to obtain that protection.
>
> [Where applicable:] Your published machine-readable standard charge file lists [code] at $[amount] for [self-pay/cash rate]. The patient was billed $[amount]. Please reconcile the difference or correct the line.
>
> Please confirm in writing: what has been sent to review, who is handling it, and the expected completion date.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Your name]

## 3 — Financial assistance application cover

Send with the completed application. The second paragraph is the one nobody uses.

> [Date]
>
> **Re: Financial assistance application — [patient name], DOB [date], Account [number]**
>
> Enclosed is my completed financial assistance application, with supporting income documentation.
>
> **Please note that Maryland law requires free medically necessary care for patients at or below 200% of the federal poverty level, and reduced-cost care below 500% where financial hardship exists.** Household size is [n] and household income is [$amount].
>
> [If applicable:] Income should be assessed as changed rather than as of the date of service. [Describe the change — job loss, reduction in hours, illness — and its date.] This occurred within **240 days of the initial bill**, which Maryland permits to be taken into account.
>
> Please also confirm in writing:
>
> 1. Whether an approval extends to the physician, radiology, anesthesia, pathology, and laboratory charges arising from the same episode, or whether separate applications are required for each.
> 2. That no interest is being charged on this account. Maryland prohibits charging interest to a patient who qualifies for free or reduced-cost care.
>
> Please confirm receipt, and tell me in writing what happens to collection activity while this application is pending.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Your name]

## 4 — Facility fee disclosure request

Maryland only. Send whenever a separate outpatient facility fee appears.

> [Date]
>
> **Re: Outpatient facility fee — Account [number], [patient name], date of service [date]**
>
> My itemized statement includes a separate outpatient facility fee of $[amount].
>
> Maryland's Facility Fee Right-to-Know Act requires that a hospital disclose outpatient facility fees **at the time the appointment is scheduled**, both orally and in writing, and obtain the patient's signed acknowledgment.
>
> Please provide a copy of the **signed acknowledgment** for this appointment, together with the written disclosure that was given and the date it was provided.
>
> If no such acknowledgment exists, please advise how the facility fee is supported on this account.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Your name]

## 5 — Insurance internal appeal

Requires the signed representative designation, not just the authorization.

> [Date]
>
> [Plan name] — Appeals Department
>
> **Re: Internal appeal — Member [name], ID [number], Claim [number], denied [date]**
>
> I am the member named above. This is a timely request for internal appeal of the denial dated [date], reason code [code].
>
> **Basis for appeal:** [State it plainly and in one paragraph. If the denial is coding or eligibility rather than medical necessity, say so and ask for reprocessing instead — it is faster and it is not an appeal.]
>
> Under the federal claims and appeals rules, please provide, free of charge:
>
> 1. The specific plan provision the denial relies on;
> 2. Any internal rule, guideline, protocol, or clinical criterion used in making the determination;
> 3. The complete claim file, including any reviewer's report or opinion.
>
> Please confirm receipt, the date the appeal was received, and the date by which a determination will be issued. If this appeal is denied, please include instructions for external review by an independent review organization.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Your name]
>
>

## 6 — HEAU complaint cover

Maryland's Attorney General mediates these free. Sent by the consumer once the file is complete — a coherent complaint is what separates yours from the pile.

> [Date]
>
> Health Education and Advocacy Unit
> Consumer Protection Division, Office of the Attorney General
>
> **Re: Request for mediation assistance — [patient name], [facility/plan]**
>
> I am the patient named above and I am requesting assistance with a billing matter I have not been able to resolve directly.
>
> **Summary:** [Two or three sentences. What was billed, what appears wrong, what was requested, what happened.]
>
> **What has already been done:**
>
> | Date | Who | What was requested | Outcome |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | [date] | [name/ref] | [itemization requested] | [received / not received] |
> | [date] | [name/ref] | [specific lines disputed, coding review requested] | [no response after [n] days] |
>
> **Enclosed:** my itemized statement, my explanation of benefits, the packet I sent on [date], and [the financial assistance determination].
>
> **What I am asking for:** that the specific entries in the enclosed packet be checked, that anything found to be in error be corrected, and that I receive a corrected statement.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Your name]

## After every letter

Diary the response date the day the consumer sends it. Chase on that date plus one. If a letter draws no response after two chases, that account has reached the next rung of the escalation ladder — move it up rather than sending a third copy of the same letter.
