# Email to the Maryland Office of Financial Regulation

**Do not send this yet.** It goes to the Office of the Commissioner of Financial Regulation at `DLFRFinReg-LABOR@maryland.gov`, from an address belonging to this business — not the vehicle-marketplace domain. Phone: 410-230-6100.

Two things changed since the first draft. The service being described is now a **free, non-representative pilot in which the consumer signs and sends everything themselves**, and the question is broken into **separate questions per activity** rather than one bundled question. A single yes/no about a bundle produces an answer that cannot be relied on for any individual part.

**Later models are separate questions.** Do not ask about a fee model or a representative model in this email. When those are contemplated, they get their own letter — an answer about a free non-representative pilot says nothing about a contingency-fee representative service.

---

**Subject:** Registration scope — Fin. Inst. Title 12, Subtitle 10 — free non-representative medical bill review pilot

Dear Office of Financial Regulation,

I am preparing to run a small, free pilot service for Maryland consumers reviewing their hospital bills, and I want to confirm before taking anyone on whether any part of it requires registration as a debt settlement services provider under Financial Institutions Article, Title 12, Subtitle 10.

**How the pilot would work, in full:**

There is **no fee of any kind** — no charge, percentage, contingency, cap, deposit, card, or success fee. No consumer funds are held, received, or disbursed at any point; consumers pay providers directly.

I would **not act as anyone's representative**. I would not contact a hospital, health plan, or collector on a consumer's behalf. I would prepare draft documents, and the consumer would review them, sign them in their own name, and send them themselves. Consumers make their own telephone calls about their own accounts.

**I would like a separate answer for each of the following activities, because I do not want to rely on a single answer covering a bundle:**

1. Reviewing a consumer's own itemized hospital bill and preparing a written list of **questions about possible discrepancies** — arithmetic, dates, quantities, and entries that appear inconsistent with the explanation of benefits — for the consumer to sign and send themselves.

2. Preparing a hospital financial assistance application under Health-General § 19-214.1 for the consumer to sign and submit themselves.

3. Preparing an internal appeal or external review request of a denied health insurance claim for the consumer to sign and file themselves.

4. Providing a consumer with a written script to use on their own telephone call about their own account, without speaking on the call.

**In no case would I renegotiate, settle, reduce, or seek to alter the terms of any debt with a creditor or debt collector**, and if a charge is confirmed correct I would not ask a provider to accept less than the amount owed.

**My questions:**

- Does any of activities 1 to 4, as described, require registration under Title 12, Subtitle 10?
- Does the answer change for any of them if a fee were charged, or if I acted as a representative? I am not proposing either now, and I would ask separately before doing so.
- Does any other Maryland registration or licence apply to any of these activities?
- Is there any guidance or advisory you would point me to?

I would rather register than get this wrong, so I am asking before I begin rather than afterwards. I am happy to provide the draft documents if that would help.

Sincerely,

[OWNER NAME]
