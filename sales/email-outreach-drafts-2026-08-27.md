## Before any send — three inputs, then one word

1. **[YOUR NAME]** — every draft signs off with a placeholder; give the name
   to sign with.
2. **[MAILING ADDRESS]** — CAN-SPAM requires a valid physical postal address
   (a PO box works) in commercial email. The drafts will not be sent with the
   placeholder in place.
3. **Sender identity** — drafts currently sit in, and would send from, the
   connected Gmail (hello@tuveloz.com). Workable for founding touches, but a
   BriefReceipt alias (Gmail Settings → Accounts → Send mail as) keeps the two
   businesses separate. Owner's call.

Then: reply **"send all"** or **"send <name>"** in the operating thread. Each
send is logged as an attempt and, on evidence of delivery, a confirmed send.
Every draft ends with an opt-out line ("reply 'no thanks' and I won't email
again") — honor it absolutely; an opt-out also removes the recipient's
follow-up.
