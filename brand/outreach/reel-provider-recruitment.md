# Provider Recruitment Reel — posting kit (pre-launch)

Phase: provider onboarding open, customer requests not yet live.
Goal: provider applications at https://tuveloz.com/join
Follows the same shared facts and honesty rules as provider-outreach-kit.md
(keep 100% of quoted price, customers pay the 5% site fee, free to join,
no exclusivity, say plainly that customer jobs aren't live yet).

## The video

AI-generated 8-second vertical clip (9:16, 1080p, ambient audio): a mobile
mechanic jump-starting an SUV in a suburban Montgomery-County-style driveway
at golden hour. No baked-in text — add all text natively in the
Instagram/TikTok editor (native text overlays index better with the
algorithm and stay repositionable per platform).

Note: it's AI-generated footage. TikTok and Instagram both require an
"AI-generated" disclosure toggle when posting realistic AI content — turn it
on. Swap in real footage of an actual local provider as soon as we have a
volunteer; real faces outperform stock-style footage for local trust.

## Why this format (the data)

- ~50% of viewers drop off in the first 3 seconds; reels with strong
  3-second hold rates reach 5–10× further. The hook text must be on screen
  at 0:00.
- 7–15 second reels get the highest completion rates, and completion rate
  outranks raw views as a distribution signal. Our 8s clip sits in the
  sweet spot — one hook, one value point, one CTA.
- Best posting windows for reels (multi-million-post studies by SocialPilot,
  Buffer, Later, Hootsuite): Monday–Thursday 8 AM–12 PM, with Wednesday and
  Thursday the strongest days; evenings 6–11 PM Wed/Thu also perform well.
