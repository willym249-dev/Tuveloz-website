# Founder counterweight clip — shoot script

The authenticity post that follows the "I've Got This" reel. That reel is
entirely AI-generated and carries the platform AI label; this one is entirely
real and carries no label, and the visible difference between the two is the
whole point. Post it within about a day of the reel so both sit near the top
of the profile together.

Companion to [reel-provider-recruitment.md](reel-provider-recruitment.md).
Everything that file says about hashtags, MoCo targeting and 48h measurement
still applies. Everything it says about production does not — this one is
deliberately unproduced.

## Hard rules

- No music, filters, colour treatment, logo, end card, tagline or burned-in
  captions. Native auto-captions only, added on the platform after upload.
- **AI disclosure toggle OFF.** Every frame is real. Leaving it off is not an
  oversight, it is the contrast this post exists to create.
- No face required. Chest-down framing throughout.
- Nothing in frame that carries someone else's trademark: tool brands
  (Snap-on, DeWalt, Milwaukee all mark the exact surfaces your hands are on),
  clothing labels, dealer plate frames. Angle them away or keep them soft.
- No licence plate, no house number, no neighbour's property in focus.
- If the hands, car or driveway belong to someone else, get a short written
  OK first. One text message saying yes is enough; nothing is not.

## Format

| | |
|---|---|
| Aspect | 9:16 vertical |
| Resolution | 4K/30 if the phone offers it, otherwise 1080p/30 |
| Length | 10–15s, one continuous handheld take |
| Audio | Natural only — wind, traffic, ratchet. No bed, no cleanup at capture. |
| Location | Real Montgomery County driveway or lot, golden hour if convenient |

## The take

```
0:00–0:01   Natural sound only. Hands already busy — sockets being sorted into
            a tray, a tool wiped down, a dipstick pulled and read, a ratchet
            set on the fender. Real motion, not mimed. Let a tool sound or the
            traffic land before any voice.

            Nothing that turns a fastener. Do not loosen or tighten anything
            on a car you do not know, and do not perform repair work you are
            not qualified to do — this shot presents the founder building
            Tuveloz, not a mechanic servicing a vehicle.

0:01–~0:10  Keep working through the whole line. Do not stop and address the
            camera. The hands carry the shot; the voice sits over them.

            "I'm building Tuveloz because good mechanics shouldn't have to
             pay just to quote a job. Provider signup is open."

            [beat — a full second, let a tool sound fill it]

            "Customer jobs aren't live yet."

~0:10       Stop on the final word. Keep rolling one extra second so the
            cut isn't tight against the audio, then stop.
```

## Why the line is first person

The voice is the founder's, so "I'm building Tuveloz" is literally true and is
much stronger than the passive alternative. It stays first person even when the
hands on camera belong to someone else — chest-down framing means the hands are
anonymous, and no claim is made about whose they are.

Only if a *different person speaks* does it become: "Tuveloz is being built so
good mechanics don't have to pay just to quote a job." Avoid that if possible.

## Do not

- Do not hire someone to appear as a mechanic. A paid person in work clothes
  beside an open hood reads as a Tuveloz provider whether or not anyone says
  so — that is invented supply, achieved by implication. There are no active
  providers yet and nothing in this post may suggest otherwise.
- Do not AI-alter a face for privacy. It looks synthetic, it triggers the AI
  label, and the label erases the exact contrast this post is buying.
- Do not brand-stamp it. The moment a lockup appears it becomes another ad.

## Shooting notes

- **Wind is the only thing that can waste the shoot.** A phone mic in open air
  turns a light breeze into a roar that buries the voice, and it will not be
  obvious until you are back inside. Stand with the wind at your back, hold the
  phone closer than feels necessary, and check the audio before leaving.
- Shoot the line **twice**, complete, no stopping mid-take. Everything else is
  fixable in the edit; unintelligible audio is not.
- **The phone has to be propped or held by someone else.** Your hands are the
  subject, so you cannot also be holding the camera. A locked-off phone on a
  small tripod at chest height is correct here; a second person holding it is
  fine too. What is not fine is a gimbal — that smoothness reads as produced.
- Speak at a normal pace. The pause before the last sentence is what makes it
  land as honesty rather than fine print — do not compress it to save a second.

## Third file: 10s of clean ambience

Separate from the two takes. Phone down, nobody talking, nobody moving — ten
unbroken seconds of the driveway as it actually sounds. Distant traffic, breeze,
whatever birds are there.

Worth more than it looks. It is room tone for this cut, and it is also a real
replacement for `sfx-ambient.mp3` in the reel build — that bed is generated,
arrived at mean -47dB, needs a +10dB boost to be audible at all, and the
question of whether it reads as outdoor air or as hiss was never settled.
Actual Montgomery County driveway tone answers that permanently and costs
nothing. If it comes back clean, drop it in as the ambient bed and rebuild.

## After the shoot

Send all three raw files, untouched — no trimming, no compression, no export
presets. Check order: voice intelligibility over wind, levels, visible
branding, plate and address, then choose the take and cut.
