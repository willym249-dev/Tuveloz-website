## The trend we're riding (as of Aug 2026)

The **"Paparazzi (Dubstep) (Slowed)"** transition — a slowed/reverb remix of
Lady Gaga's "Paparazzi" (Alximo). It's one of the month's biggest formats.

How the trend works: you open on your worst angle — bad lighting, flat stare,
on-screen text like "my honest reaction to ___" — then the beat DROPS and you
hard-cut to a polished, cool-toned "glow-up" version synced to the drop. It's
self-aware and the drop makes the switch hit hard.

Why it fits us: the whole pitch is a glow-up — grinding on somebody else's
route → running your own book. We make the *provider's independence* the
glow-up. Bonus: it works with **no face in frame** (like our best-performing
dead-battery reel) by using a car "before/after" as the transition.

Source: trend verified trending Aug 2026 — see notes at bottom.

**Option A — no face, car glow-up (recommended, matches our winner):**

## Why this format (the data)

- ~50% of viewers drop in the first 3s; the hook text must be on screen at
  0:00. A transition trend front-loads curiosity ("react to *what*?"), which
  protects the 3-second hold.
- The beat-drop cut is a built-in retention spike — completion rate outranks
  raw views as a distribution signal, and a hard transition pulls viewers to
  the drop.
- Keep it **7–12s**: one before, one drop, one CTA. Longer dilutes the trend.
- Best windows (same as our other reels): Wed 7:30 PM & Thu 12 PM for reach;
  Thu 9 AM for the conversion-minded post. After 2–3 posts, move slots to your
  real audience peaks in the Instagram Professional Dashboard.

## Guardrails (do not break)

- Provider messaging ONLY. Never imply customers can book today. The glow-up
  subject is the *provider's independence*, not "get your car detailed now."
- Launch services only in any shot: battery/jump start, detailing, wipers,
  fluids, diagnostics. No towing, tires, or AC.
- Never say "keep 95%." Providers keep 100% of their quote; the customer pays
  the site's 5% fee.
