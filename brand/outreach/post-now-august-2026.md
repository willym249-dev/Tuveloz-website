# What to post right now — August 2026

- **Status:** active
- **Owner:** hello@tuveloz.com
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-07

A dated, ready-to-post shortlist for Instagram and TikTok, built on the trend
read below and on what is already written in `audience-growth-playbook.md`
(strategy), `provider-outreach-kit.md` (1:1 DMs), `provider-spotlight-kit.md`
(the highest-yield format), and `../social-media-kit/profile-copy.md` (bios).
Those files hold the strategy; **this file holds the queue.** When the trends
below go stale, replace this file rather than adding a fourth one.

The honesty rules in `provider-outreach-kit.md` apply to every line here:
never imply customers can book today, never promise income or job volume,
never astroturf, and label AI creative on every platform that asks.

---

## 1. The constraint that decides everything

**Customer job posting is off.** `lib/launch-status.ts` sets
`CUSTOMER_JOB_POSTING_PAUSED = true` and `MARKETPLACE_MODE = "onboarding_only"`.
A customer who sees a great reel today, taps through, and finds they cannot
post a job is not a lead — they are a person who now believes the site is
broken, and they are the hardest audience to win back at launch.

So every post below is aimed at **providers**, and the one measurable outcome
is an application at `tuveloz.com/join`. Customer-facing content earns its
place only when it is useful on its own terms — a Maryland inspection fact is
worth saving whether or not the reader ever books anything — and never when it
implies a booking that cannot happen.

This is not a permanent rule. It flips the week customer requests open.

What is actually moving this month, and whether it is usable here.

| Trend | Shape | Use it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **"Preparing for my Netflix documentary"** | Sit in a chair, clip on an imaginary mic, deliver a straight-faced confession. Started with @borderbandittx on 2026-06-16, spread via @wassupa1ex on 6-30, now cross-platform | **Yes — top pick.** See §3.1 |

| **"You Never Take Me to Bangladesh"** absurdist substitution | Non-sequitur audio bit | **No** — burns credibility with a trade audience we are asking to trust us with their business |
| **"August" (Taylor Swift)** seasonal nostalgia | Summer recaps, photo dumps | **No** — nothing to recap pre-launch |

Two mechanics matter more than any individual sound:

- **Sends are weighted 3–5× likes** on Instagram for reaching new people, and
  watch-time-to-completion beats raw length — a fully-watched 30s clip
  out-performs a 60s clip abandoned at 10s. Write for *"send this to your
  cousin who fixes cars"*, not for likes.
- **Watermarks tank reach.** Never export through TikTok and re-upload to
  Reels. Export clean, upload natively to each, add text in each app's own
  editor. Accounts posting 10+ reposts in 30 days get dropped from
  recommendations entirely.

Geotag every post to a specific MoCo town — Silver Spring, Wheaton, Rockville,
Gaithersburg, Germantown — not to "Maryland". Local reach is the entire point;
a view from outside the county is worth approximately nothing here.

## 3. The queue — post these, in this order

Four posts over two weeks. Each is one vertical video reused on both platforms,
shot once. Hashtag blocks and the AI-label rule are already in
`media/captions-v3-copy-paste.txt` — reuse them, rotating a few tags per post
so the feed does not see an identical block twice.

### 3.1 — "Preparing for my Netflix documentary" (post first)

The strongest match available. It is a confession format, and this account has
a genuine confession to make: **the marketplace is not open yet.** Saying that
inside the joke is more disarming than saying it in a disclaimer, and it makes
the pre-launch status an asset instead of an apology.

Single take, phone on a box, a plain chair, natural light. No music needed —
the format carries itself on delivery, which also sidesteps §6 entirely.

> **On-screen text:** "Preparing for my Netflix documentary about launching a
> mechanic marketplace with zero customers"
>
> *[sit down, adjust an imaginary lapel mic, look just off-camera]*
>
> "People ask when we're launching. And I tell them the truth, which is that I
> won't open it to customers until enough real mechanics have signed up that a
> customer who posts a job actually gets a quote back."
>
> *[beat]*
>
> "So right now my entire job is finding mechanics in Montgomery County. That's
> it. That's the documentary."

**Caption (EN):** Not open to customers yet — on purpose. A marketplace where
you post a job and nobody answers is worse than no marketplace. So we're
signing up independent providers first. Free to join, you set your own prices,
you keep 100% of what you quote, no exclusivity. Montgomery County, MD →
tuveloz.com/join?src=doc-en

**Spanish version:** shoot it as its own take, not as subtitles on the English
one — a dubbed-looking clip reads as inauthentic in exactly the audience the
playbook calls the most underpriced channel available. Link:
`tuveloz.com/join?src=doc-es`

### 3.2 — Provider spotlight (post the moment one provider says yes)

`provider-spotlight-kit.md` already calls this the highest-yield content the
account will ever run, and the trend read backs that up: a real face in a real
driveway *is* the low-production authentic format everyone else is imitating.
One photo and three questions. The provider shares it, their family shares it,
their existing customers share it — local followers arriving pre-qualified.

Follow the consent rules in that kit. Do not fake it, and do not run a
composite "typical provider" — a spotlight of someone who does not exist is
astroturf with better production values.

Link: `tuveloz.com/join?src=spot-01` (increment per spotlight).

### 3.3 — "Worth every penny" — MoCo car edition

Rides the list trend and is genuinely useful, so it earns saves and sends from
car owners while staying honest about launch state. This is the one
customer-facing post in the queue; it asks for nothing.

> "Things that are worth every penny if you own a car in Montgomery County"
> — a battery tested before winter, not after
> — the tire shop that actually torques to spec
> — knowing what a job should cost *before* you call
> — a mechanic who tells you what you *don't* need

**Caption:** No pitch on this one. We're building a local marketplace for
vehicle services in MoCo and it isn't open to customers yet. Follow along.
Fix cars for a living? → tuveloz.com/join?src=penny

### 3.4 — Price transparency card

Pillar 3 from the playbook, and the highest-save format the account has: *"What
a battery swap should actually cost in Montgomery County."* Static text card is
fine — not everything needs to be video, and this is the kind of thing people
screenshot.

Stay inside the launch services in `docs/PITCH.md` (battery and jump start,
wipers and bulbs, fluid top-off, detailing, basic diagnostics). Do not quote
towing, tires, or A/C — they are not live. **Never present a number as a
Tuveloz price**: providers set their own prices and the platform never sets or
caps a quote. Frame it as a survey of local shop pricing, sourced and dated.

Link: `tuveloz.com/join?src=price-battery`

## 4. How to actually test this (and what was broken)

**What was broken.** The playbook's §6 says to watch "provider applications, by
source." That was not measurable. `analytics_events.props` accepted arbitrary
JSON and the A/B variant system wrote copy variants into it, but nothing ever
recorded *where a visitor came from* — so every application looked identical
regardless of which post produced it. Platform-side view counts cannot close
that gap: they can tell you a reel was watched, never that a watcher applied.

**What changed.** `lib/campaign-source.ts` now reads a `?src=` tag off the
inbound link, stores it first-touch in localStorage, and stamps it onto
`provider_signup_started` and `provider_signup_completed`. The owner funnel at
`/admin/analytics-funnel` gained a "Where applications came from" table showing
opened / submitted / conversion per tag, in the same 7-day, 30-day and all-time
windows as everything else. First touch wins deliberately: someone who sees a
reel, leaves, and returns directly a week later is still that reel's
application.

**Tag every link.** Put `?src=` on the link in every bio, every caption, every
DM, every flyer QR code. An untagged link is an application you cannot credit
to anything — those land in the `untagged` row, which is shown rather than
hidden so the numbers still reconcile.

Suggested tags: `bio-ig`, `bio-tt`, `doc-en`, `doc-es`, `spot-01`, `penny`,
`price-battery`, `dm`, `flyer`, `reddit`.

### The two-week test

Run one variable at a time. With volume this low, anything else is noise
wearing a lab coat.

- **Week 1 — format.** Post 3.1 and 3.3. Same links, same posting slots,
  different formats. Read `submitted` per source, not `opened`, and not views.
- **Week 2 — language.** Post the EN and ES cuts of 3.1 in the same slot on
  consecutive days. This is the one the playbook predicts will surprise you.

**Read it honestly.** Below roughly 30 visitors per arm you are reading noise —
the funnel page says as much on the experiments table and the same applies
here. Two weeks at this scale will not produce a statistically clean answer;
it will produce a *direction*, which is what is actually needed before spending
money in §6. If a post drives views and no applications, the content is
reaching the wrong county, not the wrong audience.

And keep the playbook's stop condition: if after four honest weeks applications
are not moving, the answer is not more posting. It is that social is the wrong
channel for this audience, and the effort belongs in 1:1 outreach and the
Google Business Profile — which is still not live, and is still the
highest-intent channel available.

## 5. What not to post

- **Anything implying a customer can book today.** The single hardest rule.
- **Any income, volume, or lead-count claim.** "Providers are getting jobs"
  is false today and would be a promise even when it isn't.
- **A fabricated provider spotlight or invented testimonial.**
- **A price presented as a Tuveloz price.** Providers set their own prices;
  showing a platform price contradicts the classification design in
  `CLAUDE.md` as well as the pitch.
- **Founding-provider perks not published on the site.** See
  `founding-provider-program.md` for what is actually published.
- **Reposts carrying another platform's watermark.**
