---

## August 2026 trend read (why this reel is shaped the way it is)

Current best-performing formats this month, and how we use each:

- **Low-production, single-take, "authentic" footage is out-reaching polished
  ads.** So the clip is one continuous hero shot, not a montage — and the plan
  is to swap in real footage of an actual local provider the moment we have a
  volunteer. Real faces beat stock-style AI for local trust.
- **Subtitle / on-screen-text driven storytelling** (the "gratitude subtitle"
  format) indexes well and needs no licensed audio. All text is added natively
  in the app editor — never baked into the export.
- **Automotive tags carry serious reach right now** — #Automotive, #CarTok,
  and #CarCommunity are pulling hundreds of millions of views. We fold a few
  of these high-reach tags into the local/intent mix below to widen the top of
  the funnel while the copy keeps us honest about pre-launch status.

## The video

AI-generated **8-second vertical clip (9:16, 1080p, ambient audio)**, one
continuous hero shot. No baked-in text — add all text natively in the
Instagram/TikTok editor (native overlays index better and stay repositionable
per platform).

Concept — "This could be your Tuesday": golden-hour, a mobile mechanic in a
clean work shirt closes the hood of an SUV in a leafy Montgomery-County-style
suburban driveway, wipes hands on a rag, gives a satisfied nod, and picks up a
tablet/phone showing a job marked complete. Warm, real, unhurried — the feeling
of finishing good work on your own schedule. Lower third stays clean and darkish
for text.

### Paste-ready generation prompt (8s, 9:16, 1080p)

> Cinematic vertical 9:16 video, 8 seconds, golden-hour late-afternoon light.
> A confident independent mobile mechanic in a clean dark work shirt closes the
> hood of a mid-size SUV parked in a leafy suburban driveway, wipes his hands on
> a shop rag, and gives a small satisfied nod to himself. He glances down at a
> phone showing a completed job. Warm natural sunlight, shallow depth of field,
> handheld documentary feel, authentic and unstaged, no text on screen. Ambient
> neighborhood audio. Lower portion of the frame kept clean and slightly shaded
> for later caption overlay. Color palette leans warm with deep-navy shadows.

Note: it's AI-generated footage. TikTok and Instagram both require an
"AI-generated" disclosure toggle when posting realistic AI content — **turn it
on** (TikTok: upload settings → AI-generated content; Instagram: Advanced
settings → Add AI label).

- Optimize **completion rate**, not raw views.
- Track link taps → `/join` visits (analytics `track` events) and actual
  applications. A reel that reaches but drives zero `/join` visits fails.
- Accounts: instagram.com/tuveloz, tiktok.com/@tuveloz, the Facebook page,
  x.com/TuvelozApp (X gets the clip as a plain video post, same caption).
