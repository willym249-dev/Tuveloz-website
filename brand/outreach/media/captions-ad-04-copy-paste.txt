=====================================================================
READ THIS FIRST — THE HASHTAG RULE CHANGED
=====================================================================
Instagram now caps hashtags at 5 per post/Reel (rolled out late 2025 into
2026), and Adam Mosseri has said plainly that hashtags are not a way to get
more reach. Instagram removed hashtag following entirely in Dec 2024.

So the 30-tag block in captions-v3-copy-paste.txt is out of date. Do not paste
it. Tags now CATEGORIZE the post; they do not boost it.

What actually drives reach on both platforms:
  - Watch time / completion. A fully-watched 9s clip beats a skipped 30s one.
  - SENDS. Weighted 3-5x likes for reaching new people. Write for "send this
    to your cousin who fixes cars."
  - A keyword-rich first line. The caption's words are searchable; spend the
    effort there, not on tags.
  - The location tag. Tag a SPECIFIC town — Silver Spring, Wheaton, Rockville,
    Gaithersburg, Germantown — never "Maryland".

5 tags, both platforms. Specific and local beats big and broad: a #MoCo view is
worth a hundred from outside the county.

=====================================================================
PICKING HASHTAGS — THE SIZE RULE
=====================================================================
The giant automotive tags are worthless to an account this size. #automotive
carries ~107M posts; #cars and #carsofinstagram are the same story. A post from
a small local account lands in that feed and is buried in seconds.

Target NICHE and MID-SIZE tags: roughly 10K–500K posts. Hyperlocal tags reported
up to 3x the engagement of broad ones for small businesses, and Instagram's own
guidance of 3–5 highly relevant tags outperforms 10+ loose ones by around 25%.

THE AUDIENCE IS PROVIDERS, NOT CUSTOMERS. That decides the whole set.

Tags split into two crowds, and only one of them is the target:
  MECHANICS TALKING TO MECHANICS  #MechanicLife  #MechanicsOfInstagram
    #MechanicsOfTikTok  #AutoTechnician  #WrenchLife  #ShopLife
    #MecanicoMovil  #TallerMovil  #MecanicoADomicilio
  CUSTOMERS AND ENTHUSIASTS  #AutoRepair  #CarTok  #CarsOfInstagram
    #CarCommunity — these reach people who want a mechanic, or who just like
    cars. Neither applies to a job posting they cannot make yet.

Spend the slots on the first crowd. #MobileMechanic straddles both and earns
its place because mechanics use it on their own work.

The shape that works here, 5 slots:
  3-4  TRADE COMMUNITY   from the first list above
  1    GEO, county-level #MontgomeryCountyMD

Only ONE geo tag, and no town tag. The post's LOCATION TAG already does the
local work — it is a stronger local signal than a hashtag and costs no slot.
Rotate the town on the location tag instead, and keep the hashtags on the trade.

NEVER use: #cars #carsofinstagram #automotive #auto #carporn #supercars
#smallbusiness — all too large to reach anyone local.

Worth remembering: hashtags are a weak channel for reaching this audience at
all. Independent mechanics in MoCo are far easier to find in local Facebook
groups, on Marketplace, and by DM — see provider-outreach-kit.md and
moco-outreach-worklist.md. Social is the supporting act here, not the plan.

Volume check: post counts move, and they cannot be read from outside the app.
Before locking a set, type each tag into Instagram search and look at the count.
Drop anything over ~1M or under ~1K — the first buries you, the second reaches
nobody.

=====================================================================
TIKTOK AUDIO — WHAT YOU MAY ACTUALLY USE
=====================================================================
These videos are ads: brand mark, value props, a CTA. That makes the audio a
COMMERCIAL use.

TikTok's general music library (chart hits, viral FYP sounds) is licensed for
personal, non-commercial use only. Using it on a promotional post risks the
audio being muted or stripped, the post being pulled on a rights claim, and
strikes accruing on the account. It also permanently blocks paid promotion —
boosting requires cleared audio.

The account-type tell: if the picker shows major-label tracks, you are on a
Personal/Creator account. A Business account shows ONLY the Commercial Music
Library, so it cannot pick wrong.

Three safe options:
  1. Commercial Music Library (1M+ tracks, cleared for business use). Verify
     with the FILTER control in the sound picker — searching the words "cml
     library" is only a text search and proves nothing.
  2. Silent. Genuinely fine; every word is on screen and it is a 9.5s read.
  3. Your own recorded audio. Fair game on any account type, and a founder
     talking over it beats a pop song for a recruitment ad.

CML picks that fit these cuts:
  - "Little Things" — Adrián Berenguer. Gentle, builds, very widely used so it
    reads native. First choice.
  - "Little Sparrow" — Paul Alan Morris. Warmer, acoustic. Use when you want it
    to feel like a person rather than a company.
  Avoid luxury/corporate beds and lifestyle-vlog beds — wrong register for
  recruiting mechanics.

Trim so the track starts on a build, not mid-phrase; aim the lift at the third
card and let it carry into the CTA. Full volume is fine — no voiceover to
compete with. Do not expect the sound to move reach: on a 9.5s post,
watch-through and sends do the work.
