$80. Or $260. Same dead battery, same car. 🔋

Here's the part nobody explains: none of those shops are lying to you.

Two shops can quote hundreds apart and both be honest — different parts
quality, different book labor time, different diagnostic fees, different
warranty. Independents bill $40–$100 an hour. Dealers bill $75–$150.
In high-cost metros like ours it runs $130–$175. On a three-hour job
that spread alone is about $300 before a single part gets priced.

So the problem was never dishonest mechanics. The problem is that you're
expected to make a $200 decision with no way to see the options side by
side — while your car sits in the driveway and you're already late.

That's the thing we're building for Montgomery County. You describe what
your vehicle needs, local independent providers send real quotes, you
compare and you pick. Your schedule, your price, your call.

Straight up: customer requests aren't live yet. We're onboarding local
providers first so there's actually something to choose from on day one.
Follow along and we'll tell you the day it opens.

Mechanics, mobile techs, detailers — you're who we're signing up right
now. Free to join, no provider fees, you set your prices and keep 100%
of what you quote: tuveloz.com/join
