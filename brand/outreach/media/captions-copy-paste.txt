WHY THESE HASHTAGS
=====================================================================
Three intents mixed on every post:
1. LOCAL   — #DMV is the big one for this region (DC/MD/VA all follow it),
             plus #DMVMechanics, #DMVsmallbusiness, #MontgomeryCountyMD
             and the individual towns so reach stays in the service area.
2. IDENTITY— #WorkForYourself, #BeYourOwnBoss, #OwnYourTime, #SkilledTrades,
             #BlueCollar: what the audience calls themselves.
3. INTENT  — #SideHustle, #SideGig, #GigWork: people actively looking for
             income opportunities.

Platform sizing: Instagram allows 30 tags and rewards a full set; TikTok
measurably deprioritizes tag-stuffed posts, so keep TikTok to ~5.
Rotate a few tags between posts so the algorithm doesn't see identical
blocks. Put the big block in the FIRST COMMENT on Instagram if you want a
cleaner-looking caption — reach is identical either way.

After 2-3 posts, check Instagram Professional Dashboard -> Total followers
-> most active times, and move the posting slots to your real audience
peaks. Your own data beats every benchmark.
