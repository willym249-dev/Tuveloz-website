=====================================================================
MUSIC / SOUND — LEGAL RULE FOR A BUSINESS
=====================================================================
Tuveloz posts are COMMERCIAL use (a business promoting a service). The
regular TikTok/Instagram music catalogs (chart songs) are licensed only
for personal posts — using them on a brand post risks a muted audio,
limited reach, or takedown.

Standing rule: only use sounds from the COMMERCIAL MUSIC LIBRARY (CML).
- TikTok: switch to a Business account (Settings -> Account -> Switch to
  Business Account) and TikTok auto-restricts you to cleared sounds. Or on
  a Creator account, filter to "Commercial sounds" when adding audio.
- Instagram: professional/business accounts get the licensed (limited)
  library for the same reason.
- The CML still has trending-style tracks — you keep the sound boost
  without the legal risk. Never upload outside music you don't have rights
  to.
