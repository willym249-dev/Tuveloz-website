# Ad 02 — "The Rescue" (30s, customer launch / coming-soon teaser)

Concept (Wil's): Aerial view, car cruising a scenic coastal road, music up.
Car breaks down — music cuts dead. Driver calls Tuveloz. Car fixed.
Music slams back in as they drive away.

Phase gate: DO NOT run as-is pre-launch (implies customers can book).
Pre-launch version: add end card "Launching soon in Montgomery County, MD —
follow along at tuveloz.com". Launch version: end card "Post your job at tuveloz.com".

Formats: 16:9 primary (YouTube/cinematic), 9:16 crop secondary.
Credits target: 0 — all shots below exist as Artlist stock.

## Music brief (the whole ad hinges on this)

Need a track with a strong, instantly-recognizable groove that survives a hard cut.
Artlist search: `feel good driving indie rock` or `funky confident road trip`.
Pick something where 0:00–0:06 already has the hook going — no slow intros.
SFX: `engine sputter stall`, `car hood slam`, `whoosh` for end card.

## The phone shot (shot 3) — zero credits

We don't need AI or a custom shoot for the Tuveloz screen. Claude composites a
real screenshot of the tuveloz.com job-post flow onto the phone in the stock
clip (or does a clean full-screen UI insert between shots 3 and 4, which is
easier and reads better on mobile).

## Notes

- Location vibe: coastal/LA reads aspirational; if we want it to feel local,
  swap searches to `aerial car scenic road trees` (very MoCo). Decision: Wil.
- If one gap can't be filled by stock (e.g. provider-van arrival), that's the
  ONE place to consider an AI video generation — last resort, after rough cut.
