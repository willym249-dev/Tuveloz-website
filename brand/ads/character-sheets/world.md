# TUV-WORLD-01 — location, light, grade

Role: the invisible continuity glue. Every shot of Ad 02 carries this block.
Consistent world hides small element drift; inconsistent world exposes
everything.

## LOCKED BLOCK (paste verbatim at the END of every shot prompt)

> Setting: a scenic two-lane road with gentle curves, guardrail on one side,
> green trees and glimpses of open water on the other, light traffic, late
> golden hour, warm low sun from screen-left, long soft shadows, clear sky
> with thin warm haze, cinematic color grade with warm highlights and soft
> teal shadows, shallow depth of field, shot on a cinema camera, no text,
> no logos, no watermarks.

Location note: this is the coastal/scenic read from Wil's concept. If the
call is made to go local-MoCo instead, swap "glimpses of open water" for
"open fields and wooded hills" — change it HERE once, never per-shot.

## What may NEVER change

- Time of day: late golden hour in every shot. The story spans ~30 minutes
  of fictional time; the sun does not move.
- Sun direction: from screen-left. Flip the shot, not the sun.
- Grade: warm highlights / soft teal shadows (matches the Ad 01 aerial).
- Weather: clear and dry. No rain, no fog — a breakdown, not a disaster.

## What MAY vary per shot

- Camera position/height (aerial for 1 and 6, ground level for 2–5)
- Which stretch of the road, as long as guardrail-and-water side stays
  consistent relative to the car's direction of travel (car always travels
  screen-left-to-right; keep it — reversing direction mid-story reads as
  driving home)

## Reference set (generate once, then freeze)

1. `ref-aerial.png` — high aerial of the road, no cars
2. `ref-ground.png` — ground-level shoulder view, guardrail visible
3. `ref-grade.png` — any frame graded to final look, used as style ref

Register in Higgsfield as style/environment ref `TUV-WORLD-01` and attach to
every generation.

## Screen direction map (whole ad)

- Shots 1, 6: car moves screen-left → screen-right
- Shot 2: car decelerates rightward, pulls to the far-side shoulder
- Shots 3–5: car parked nose-right on the shoulder, van parked behind it
  (screen-left of the car), water side beyond the guardrail
