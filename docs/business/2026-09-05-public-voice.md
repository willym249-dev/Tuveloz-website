# Tuveloz wording research

- **Status:** active
- **Owner:** hello@tuveloz.com
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-05
- **Applies to:** public marketing copy and provider recruitment

Reviewed September 5, 2026. These are established service marketplaces; this research does not establish that any particular phrase causes higher conversion.

| Source | Useful pattern | Application to Tuveloz |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Taskrabbit provider page](https://www.taskrabbit.com/become-a-tasker) | Starts with providers' control over rates, schedule and work area, then explains registration. | Welcome skilled local people, explain control over prices and hours, and make applying feel manageable. |
| [Thumbtack: how it works](https://www.thumbtack.com/how-it-works) | Uses brief, concrete steps and familiar verbs. | Explain describing the need, comparing quotes and deciding. Use buttons that name the actual next step. |
| [Thumbtack for professionals](https://www.thumbtack.com/pro) | Names practical benefits and gives the signup invitation prominence. | Put free applications, no lead fees and independent schedules near the invitation. Do not borrow its demand statistics or imply equivalent customer volume. |
| [YourMechanic customer support](https://www.yourmechanic.com/support) | Answers practical concerns about pricing, parts, timing and where work can happen. | Explain the planned customer fee and what a quote includes, and make it easy to ask the owner a question. Do not borrow its warranty, geographic coverage or price promises. |

The proposed voice is neighborly, respectful and clear. Recognize that car trouble can be stressful and that providers have invested care in their trade. Offer an understandable next step. Keep promises specific and backed by the actual product.

Examples in the revised copy:

- Customer welcome: **Car care should feel less stressful.**
- Provider welcome: **Your skills belong here.**
- Provider invitation: **Do you work on cars, offer detailing, or run a mobile service or shop? We'd like to hear from you.**
- Help: **Have a question? You're welcome to ask.** The following paragraph identifies the automated guide and explains how to contact the owner.
- Account button: **Create a free account.** Provider button: **Apply free.**

Removed unsupported activity and priority claims, unconditional price promises, blanket free-service wording, and invented business names in sample quote cards. The cards remain clearly labeled as previews. The founding program keeps its published benefits and acceptance rules, with wording that does not imply a full cohort has already joined.

English and Spanish receive matching meaning, with natural Spanish phrasing instead of literal slogan translations. Logos and icons remain deferred as requested. The existing page structure is retained; this is a copy improvement, not a visual redesign.

Measure results using actual application starts and completed submissions. Existing A/B totals include earlier wording; they cannot establish the conversion performance of the September 5 rewrite by themselves. A fresh, separately attributable comparison would be needed before calling either headline a winner.
