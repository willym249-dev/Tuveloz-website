# TUVELOZ integrated review checklist

## Current status

This is a combined review build. It is not legal advice, proof of legal compliance, or approval to launch the marketplace.

TUVELOZ's owner may elect to proceed without hiring private counsel. Hiring counsel is therefore a recommendation, not a required proof field or a substitute for the owner's launch decision. That choice does not waive, reduce, or postpone any applicable federal, Maryland, local, license, registration, permit, insurance, tax, payment, privacy, security, consumer-protection, or service-specific requirement. TUVELOZ must not claim that an attorney approved the site or that the company has zero liability.

The safety position is intentionally conservative:

- Real customer job submissions are paused.
- Only isolated, persisted test jobs can enter the job-operations workflow.
- Live Stripe charges and provider transfers are disabled in code and configuration.
- Every service is default-deny until that exact service has current mandatory-compliance, government/agency, insurance, and provider evidence.
- TUVELOZ does not employ, train, assign, or require providers to accept work.
- Employee and trainee pathways cannot receive work until a provider-of-record assignment model is designed and approved.

## Included in this review build

### Provider onboarding and verification

- Guided provider signup with separate independent-startup, sponsored-trainee, and provider-employee pathways.
- Exact-service selection; there is no broad "general mechanic" approval.
- Private evidence uploads with correction and replacement history.
- Quarantine until an authenticated external malware scanner reports a matching clean result.
- Evidence review by exact document, service, jurisdiction, issuer, dates, and scope.
- Checkmark/X eligibility with plain-language reasons.
- Expiration reminders at 60, 30, 14, 7, and 1 day.
- Provider appeals and privacy/data-rights requests.
- Immutable Provider Agreement acceptance records.
- Automatic routing only for an eligible independent owner-operator; sponsored workers remain blocked.
- Legacy/test-provider tools cannot promote a fixture into a real or publicly verified provider.

### Customer request and booking review

- One exact service code per request.
- Exact operation code plus affirmative confirmation that every prohibited operation is absent.
- Structured address/location type, property permission, jurisdiction, municipality, and schedule.
- Vehicle driveability, condition, high-voltage status, and service-specific safety confirmations.
- Separate Terms/Customer Agreement and Privacy acknowledgements.
- Exact quote, provider, scope, schedule, price, policy version, and customer-authorization snapshots.
- Eligibility rechecks at quote, booking, job start, scope change, completion, and payout.
- Actual start requires a fresh provider-arrival location, address, vehicle-condition, high-voltage, prohibited-operation, and safety snapshot that matches the accepted scope.

### Test-only job operations

- Cancellation and no-show records.
- Incidents, claims, and stop-work controls.
- Atomic change-order acceptance or decline.
- Added work cannot begin without exact-service eligibility and customer authorization.
- The latest authorized labor, parts, taxes/other charges, TUVELOZ fee, and total flow through customer display, checkout snapshot, and payout review.
- An open test Checkout Session is expired before a price/scope change can commit; stale sessions and paid webhooks cannot bind to the new scope.
- Delayed work is checked at the actual start time, not only the original schedule.
- Invoices, warranty records, refund requests and decisions, disputes, reserves, holds, and payout review.
- No job-completion event automatically releases money.

### Owner review center

- Provider compliance queue.
- Appeals, reminders, privacy requests, legal holds, and deletion-evidence queue.
- Exact service-activation approvals with expiration controls.
- Launch-readiness dashboard with separate evidence for every named approving authority.
- Test job-operations console.
- Audit records for sensitive owner actions.

## Outside requirements, approvals, and systems still missing

These cannot be truthfully checked off by software alone:

- TUVELOZ adopts final Customer Terms, Provider Agreement, Privacy/Cookie Notice, Payment/Cancellation/Refund Policy, and Conduct/Review Policy that comply with every applicable mandatory requirement. Independent professional legal review is recommended for complex or disputed provisions, but attorney approval is not being treated as a launch proof requirement.
- TUVELOZ documents the applicable Maryland repair-facility duties, assigns them consistently in the real workflow, and confirms that the exact first-launch service matrix, public claims, badges, screening position, and dispute language comply with all mandatory laws and government rules.
- An insurance broker/carrier binds TUVELOZ coverage and confirms the required provider coverage for each exact service.
- A real provider identity and business-verification process is selected and tested.
- A malware-scanning vendor is selected, connected to private uploads, and tested end to end.
- Stripe approves the actual marketplace/Connect model and the final payment, refund, reserve, dispute, and payout design.
- A CPA or qualified tax adviser approves the fee, tax, bookkeeping, income-recognition, information-reporting, and transaction map.
- Required Maryland/local government registrations, permits, licenses, and direct issuer-verification methods are confirmed for each enabled service and location.
- TUVELOZ decides whether to use third-party background/consumer reports. If it does, the full legally required FCRA disclosure, authorization, pre-adverse, dispute, and final-action workflow must be implemented and tested first. Independent professional review is strongly recommended for that workflow.
- The provider-of-record model for a provider business's employees or trainees is designed, documented against applicable employment, tax, insurance, and repair requirements, insured, and approved. Until then, those people cannot receive jobs.
- Reminder delivery, escalation staffing, incident response, legal-notice ownership, and support procedures are operational.
- Production Cloudflare secrets, Access configuration, D1/R2 backups, remote migrations, monitoring, and deployment are verified.

## Decisions to review and tweak

- Which exact services and Maryland locations launch first.
- Minimum provider documents, insurance limits, verification source, renewal timing, and approved badge wording for each service.
- Whether the proposed customer fee remains 10% and how taxes and processing costs are handled.
- Cancellation/no-show amounts, refund waterfall, chargeback allocation, reserves, negative balances, and payout timing.
- Parts ownership, returned-parts rules, provider/manufacturer warranties, and whether TUVELOZ offers any separate guarantee.
- Customer and provider support contacts, response deadlines, incident escalation, claim handoff, and appeal deadlines.
- Final privacy retention/deletion periods, cookie/analytics choices, and vendor inventory.
- Whether independent startups are the only initial provider pathway.

## Suggested review order

1. Review public wording and every policy page for the same marketplace model and every applicable mandatory requirement.
2. Walk through provider onboarding, evidence correction, expiration, appeal, and X/checkmark states.
3. Walk through the exact customer request and quote-acceptance screens.
4. Use a persisted test provider/job to test start, delay, change order, incident, invoice, refund, and payout holds.
5. Review all owner queues and confirm no single approval can bypass an exact-service requirement.
6. Give the owner, broker/carrier, CPA or tax adviser, Stripe, government/agency contacts, and security/scanner reviewer the matching evidence fields in the launch-readiness dashboard. Add independent counsel as an optional reviewer if retained.
7. Re-run the complete build/test suite after every policy or workflow change.

## Release boundary

Do not turn on real jobs or payments merely because this review build passes its tests. Full marketplace activation remains blocked until every applicable mandatory requirement and required outside approval is current, the exact first-launch services are enabled individually, production systems are verified, and a final end-to-end launch review passes. Choosing not to hire counsel does not satisfy any of those requirements by itself.
