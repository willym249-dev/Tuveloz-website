# Legal and compliance documents

Policy research, agreement drafts, regulatory analysis, and the reasoning behind
compliance decisions.

Nothing filed yet.

**The published legal documents are not here.** All seven — Terms of Service,
Customer Agreement, Provider Agreement, Privacy Notice, Payment Policy,
Marketplace Conduct, and the Provisional Provider Policy — are pages under
`app/`, each pinned to a reviewed content hash in `config/policy-releases.json`.
Editing one of those pages without recording a new release fails the build. That
is deliberate: published legal text should not change quietly.

What belongs here instead is the work behind those pages — research into
Maryland repair-duty rules, insurance requirement analysis, drafts under review,
and notes from legal counsel.

Certificates, filings, and licenses are tracked as record cards in
[`../records/`](../records/), not as documents here.

See [`../FILING-GUIDE.md`](../FILING-GUIDE.md) for naming and the header block
every document needs, and add a row to [`../README.md`](../README.md) when you
file something.
