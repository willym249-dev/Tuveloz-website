## What it covers

Legal existence, the liability shield, and the authority to contract as a
business. Also the name itself: the entity name is what appears on the Provider
Agreement and every customer-facing legal page.

Related but **separate documents**, each wanting its own card once it exists:

- Operating agreement — who owns the company and who may bind it. This is the
  "ownership authority" half of the entity gate, and articles of organization do
  not establish it
- EIN assignment — see [`ein-assignment.md`](ein-assignment.md)
- Trade name filing, if the business operates under anything other than the
  registered entity name

## What depends on it

- `entity_authority_domain_and_code` — the LLC-records and ownership-authority
  parts
- `provider_identity_and_business_verification`, which verifies providers against
  Maryland business records; the platform's own standing sits behind it
- The Provider Agreement and Customer Agreement, both of which name the entity

## Reminder

The annual report deadline is the one that bites — it is easy to miss and
forfeiture is the consequence. Set a reminder when you fill this in, and note it
here.
